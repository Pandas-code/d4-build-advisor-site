Perma-Berserk Rend Barbarian

melee bleed / cooldown-uptime

Barbarian theorycraft unchanged melee bleed / cooldown-uptime confidence medium

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 14

Move on whenReach level 70 and clear Pit Tier 10 for Torment 1 - the same gate as any Barbarian. Swap Call of the Ancients for Wrath of the Berserker here and start the actual experiment.
Move on whenWrath of the Berserker uptime measured at 60% or better in a real Pit run. If it is materially below that, this build's premise has failed - abandon it and play barbarian-meta-whirlwind instead.
Move on whenWrath of the Berserker uptime at or above 70% and glyphs levelled evenly - only then is the build worth taking into pushing.

Skill bar

1Lunging Strike
2Rend
3Ground Stomp
4War Cry
5Challenging Shout
6Call of the Ancients

Rotation

Ground Stomp to gather and stun, War Cry, then Rend across the whole pack and move on while the bleeds tick. Do not stand and re-apply on a single target.

Skill point priority

  1. Rend to max - it is the damage skill for the whole build
  2. War Cry for the multiplier and Berserking
  3. Lunging Strike for Fury while you still need it
  4. Challenging Shout and Ground Stomp for survival
  5. Call of the Ancients while levelling; it swaps out for Wrath of the Berserker the moment you start gearing for cooldown

Rotation

Same loop. This is the stage where the Melted Heart of Selig decision has to be made and it is a genuine fork: the Mythic doubles your resource pool and turns it into your life pool, which demands stripping all Life affixes, but this build deliberately does not care about Fury. Test both - a plain amulet with a greater-affix cooldown roll may simply be better here, which would be a real finding.

Skill point priority

  1. Rend and Wrath of the Berserker maxed
  2. War Cry maxed
  3. Every remaining point into defensive passives - this build has no Iron Skin slot yet
  4. Warbringer for Fortify uptime

Summary

UNTESTED - this is an original theorycraft, not a build any source has ranked in this exact form. The idea: 3.1.0 both capped Fury generation (0.5 Fury per 1% Maximum Life, and no longer helped by Resource Generation bonuses) and gutted Aspect of Limitless Rage from 1-1.5% to 0.15-0.20% damage per Fury, while simultaneously turning Wrath of the Berserker into a flat +30% all damage for its duration. Those two changes point in opposite directions, and every published Barbarian build still gears for Fury. This build gears for cooldown reduction instead and pairs it with Rend, a bleed that ticks over time and therefore spends more of its damage inside a persistent multiplier window than a burst skill would. Rend is independently credible - Maxroll has it S tier on the push list and A tier on the overall endgame list, and its week-1 review notes Rend unexpectedly dominating Tower leaderboards - but the cooldown-first gearing is mine and has not been validated by anyone.

Reasoning

HypothesisBecause 3.1.0 capped Fury generation and reduced Aspect of Limitless Rage's per-Fury damage by roughly 85%, the marginal damage of Fury investment has collapsed, while Wrath of the Berserker's new flat +30% all damage is uncapped by resource. Therefore stacking cooldown reduction to hold Wrath of the Berserker near-permanently should beat Fury-centric gearing, and the gain should be larger on Rend than on a burst skill because bleed damage ticks continuously and so captures a higher fraction of a persistent multiplier.
Confidencemedium
How to test itMeasure Wrath of the Berserker uptime as a percentage over a full Pit run - if you cannot clear roughly 70% uptime, the premise fails and you should revert to the standard Whirlwind gearing. Then run the same Pit tier twice: once in the cooldown-stacked setup below, once with Fury Regeneration and Resource Cost Reduction in those affix slots, and compare clear times rather than tooltip numbers. Watch specifically for (a) whether Rend's bleed damage snapshots the Wrath of the Berserker multiplier at application or recalculates per tick - if it snapshots, the whole thesis weakens because you can just apply bleeds inside a short window; (b) whether Fury starvation makes Rend uncastable often enough to erase the gain, since you have deliberately given up Fury Regeneration; and (c) whether Ramaladni's Magnum Opus, which scales with maximum Fury, is worth more than the cooldown amulet despite the thesis. Report back with the uptime number before trusting anything else here.
