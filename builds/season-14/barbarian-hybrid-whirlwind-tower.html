How to run them

Same pairing as the base build - Raheir hired with Ground Slam, Bastion, Inspiration and Raheir's Aegis, Aldkin reinforcing with Field of Languish - and on this variant the choice is less optional than it is on the base build. Tower floors kill you through incoming damage rather than through a damage check, so Raheir's Valiance cheat-death and Aldkin's area damage reduction are both doing real work. Icy Veins' Varyana/Bloodthirst reinforcement is the offensive alternative and is the wrong side of the trade here: this variant already gave up Lunging Strike for Iron Skin on the same reasoning.

Plan

Same system and the same caveat as the base build: Maxroll ships one generic default War Plans tree for every Season 14 Whirlwind profile and explicitly tells you to adapt it. The adaptation for a Tower variant is to weight The Pit and Nightmare Dungeons over the material-farming trees, because what gates a Tower push is glyph level, not gear acquisition.

Summary

The meta Whirlwind build, retuned for Tower and high Pit tiers rather than general farming. Two facts from the knowledge base drive every change: Maxroll's week-1 review reports that Rend and Flame Charge - not Whirlwind - are the Barbarian builds dominating Tower leaderboards, and Maxroll's push list puts five Barbarian builds in S tier while its overall endgame list puts three. So the same class carries you either way, but the tuning differs. This variant keeps the Whirlwind engine (which you have already geared) and shifts affixes, defensive slots and the Mythic choice toward surviving and sustaining at push tiers, where a single mistake ends the run. Take this only after barbarian-meta-whirlwind is fully geared - it is a retune, not a fresh character.

Reasoning

Based onWhirlwind Barbarian
Tweaks vs. the base build
  1. Drop Lunging Strike for Iron Skin from early_endgame onward, not just at the pushing stage. Reason: the base build keeps Lunging Strike as a Fury fallback, but 3.1.0 capped Fury generation at 0.5 Fury per 1% Maximum Life and cut it off from Resource Generation bonuses, so the fallback returns less than it used to while the survivability slot is worth strictly more at push tiers.
  2. Prioritise Cooldown Reduction over Attack Speed on gloves and amulet. Reason: Wrath of the Berserker now grants a flat +30% all damage for its duration in 3.1.x, so uptime on that one button is a bigger multiplier than incremental spin speed once your attack-speed breakpoint is already met.
  3. Keep Melted Heart of Selig as the crafted Mythic and be strict about it: zero Life affixes anywhere on gear. Reason: the Mythic doubles your resource pool and converts it into your effective life pool, so Life rolls dilute it. Season 14 allows only ONE crafted Mythic equipped at a time, so this is the whole crafted-Mythic budget - do not craft a second and expect to use both.
  4. Slot dropped Mythic weapons (The Grandfather or Ramaladni's Magnum Opus) rather than crafting them. Reason: the one-crafted-Mythic limit applies only to crafted items - dropped Mythics are unlimited, and the Corrupted Reaper in the Pandemonium Threshold has the best Mythic drop rates in the game. Since patch 3.1.2 Mythics can also be recycled, so a wrong craft is recoverable.
  5. Farm Superior Lair Keys deliberately instead of opportunistically, and run Ruptures in Helltide zones rather than in the Pit. Reason: Ruptures spawn most often in Helltide zones and their spawn frequency was explicitly decreased in the Pit, so Pit runs are for pushing, not for seasonal loot.
  6. Do not slot Aspect of Walloping and do not build into 2-Handed Techniques. Reason: Walloping is confirmed non-functional with the Whirlwind Tornado upgrade, and 2-Handed Techniques are partially or wholly broken this patch. This matters more here than in the base build because push builds have no slack for a dead multiplier.
  7. Keep a Rend respec saved. Reason: Rend is S tier on the push list and is what is actually topping Tower leaderboards; if your Whirlwind clear stalls two tiers below your Pit ceiling, the class is not the problem and swapping skills is cheaper than regearing.
