werewolf melee / storm hybrid

Druid meta unchanged werewolf melee / storm hybrid

Move on whenReach the point where you can equip Berú of the Red Wolf Moon - it is the documented stand-in until Berú of the Storm Shepherd drops, and the build changes character once a Berú is online.

Rotation

Toggle Lightning Storm and Hurricane on. Enter Grizzly Rage whenever available. Cast Debilitating Roar before it expires. Then click Shred as fast as you can - do not hold the button, because rapid casts let Waxing Gibbous's extra attacks re-trigger the 2-set bonus. Runes: PocQue for the Earthen Bulwark barrier and Unstoppable, which feeds Tibault's Will.

Skill point priority

  1. Shred max
  2. Hurricane max - it applies Weaken, restores Spirit and grants movement speed, so it is doing three jobs
  3. Lightning Storm - kept mainly as the trigger for Aspect of the Orange Herald rather than for its own damage
  4. Grizzly Rage max for the damage multiplier
  5. Debilitating Roar for defence and the Dark Howl bonus damage
  6. Earthen Bulwark via the Que rune for Barrier and Unstoppable

Rotation

Rotation

Group with Azurewrath, Grizzly Rage, then click Shred rapidly through the pack while Hurricane and Lightning Storm tick. War Plans priority for farming your keys: Writhe and Rot at Level 2 Helltide for Hellwyrm experience and Lair Keys, then Grigoire's Gambit at Level 1 for early Legendary Aspects - and avoid Cult of Annihilation on Hardcore, where Doomsayers have caused instant deaths.

Skill point priority

  1. Shred, Hurricane and Grizzly Rage maxed
  2. Debilitating Roar is not optional at this tier - Druid survivability is the class's named weakness and this is your mitigation
  3. Earthen Bulwark on the Que rune for the Unstoppable, which also feeds Tibault's Will
  4. Defensive passives over the last marginal damage nodes

How to run them

Every one of the seven planner profiles hires Raheir and takes Ground Slam, Bastion, Iron Wolf's Virtue and Raheir's Aegis - note Iron Wolf's Virtue, which is where the Druid differs from the Whirlwind Barbarian's Inspiration. What you are buying is Valiance, a rotating cheat-death, and this build needs it: survivability is the acknowledged Druid weakness this season. Reinforce with Varyana on Bloodthirst for attack speed, which on a build whose damage comes from clicking Shred as fast as possible is a direct damage gain. Maxroll frames the reinforcement as an explicit either/or and Icy Veins takes the other branch: Aldkin with Field of Languish, triggered by Shred, for damage reduction instead. Take Aldkin if you are dying; the hybrid variant of this build does exactly that.

Plan

War Plans is the Lord of Hatred endgame playlist: at Tyrael's table in Temis you chain up to five endgame activities, each of the seven activities carries its own seven-level tree, and those trees only earn experience while the activity is part of an active plan. Boards are per-character with three redraws. This build is the one case where the source actually publishes build-specific advice: the Maxroll planner's War Plans tree is genuinely tuned for the Shred Druid rather than copied from the generic default - its Whispers tree differs entirely from the Barbarian's, Helltide swaps Meat Brothers for Wretched Vermin, and The Pit swaps Choron's Shortcut for Choron's Soul. One hard warning from the guide: avoid Cult of Annihilation on Hardcore, where its Doomsayers have caused instant deaths.

Summary

The Druid meta build, and the clearest example this season of an item-driven rather than skill-driven meta. Shred's damage is carried by the Berú of the Storm Shepherd set, whose bonus scales damage directly with your Spirit regeneration rate - which means regen affixes are damage affixes and the whole gearing logic inverts from a normal build. Cross-checked: Icy Veins rates Shred S tier, Maxroll's push list rates it S tier, and the freshest patch-3.1.2 class snapshot says Storm Shred remains viable after the 3.1.1 adjustments. Disagreements kept visible: Maxroll's overall endgame list rates Shred only A tier (it has no Druid build in S), so this is a stronger pushing build than a general farming build. Two honest warnings before you commit: survivability is Druid's named weakness this season, and Maxroll attributes part of the Storm Shepherd set's dominance to bugs - including the fact that clicking Shred beats holding it, which they describe as a mechanical exploit. This is the highest nerf-risk meta build of the three deep-dive classes.
