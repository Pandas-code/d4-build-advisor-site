Poison Penetrating Shot Rogue

ranged piercing bow

Rogue meta unchanged ranged piercing bow

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 14

Move on whenLevel 70 with Torment 1 unlocked - the endgame guide assumes exactly this starting point. Aspect of Imitated Imbuement must be in hand before you make the switch.
Move on whenYou have the Sightless Set assembled - once Shadow Clone starts resetting rapidly the rotation changes character entirely.
Move on whenEaglehorn equipped and the Sightless Set complete - at that point start Pit and Tower pushing rather than farming.

Rotation

Poison Imbuement up, Forceful Arrow to refill Energy, then Penetrating Shot down the longest line of enemies you can find. Dash between packs. Shadow Clone on every elite.

Skill point priority

  1. Penetrating Shot to max - it is the only damage skill that matters
  2. Forceful Arrow for Energy generation; the build is Energy-hungry from the very start
  3. Poison Imbuement next - its cooldown shortens as you land hits, so uptime comes from attacking
  4. Dash and Shadow Step for mobility and repositioning
  5. Shadow Clone as the ultimate: it applies Vulnerable and Weaken and resets cooldowns

Rotation

Keep Poison Imbuement up by hitting things, not by waiting on the cooldown. Shadow Clone for the damage window and the Vulnerable application. Dash between packs to stack Ferocity. Penetrating Shot continuously down enemy lines. Talisman at this stage: Spellbound Steel, which applies Shadow Imbuement while you progress toward the real set.

Skill point priority

  1. Penetrating Shot max
  2. Poison Imbuement max - the Poison variant's damage identity
  3. Shadow Clone max: it is the Poison variant's Vulnerable source, which is a different role than it plays in the Cold variant
  4. Concealment for Stealth, Unstoppable and Ferocity stacking
  5. Dash for Ferocity stacks and mobility
  6. Inner Sight specialization to help with Energy

Rotation

With the Sightless Set online, Shadow Clone comes back almost immediately - so lead every pack with it rather than saving it. Poison Imbuement is maintained by attacking. Note the Mythic budget: only ONE crafted Mythic can be equipped at a time, so Eaglehorn is the craft and everything else on this list has to drop. Since 3.1.2 you can recycle Mythics, so a wrong craft is recoverable.

Skill point priority

  1. Penetrating Shot, Poison Imbuement and Shadow Clone maxed
  2. Concealment kept for Ferocity and the Unstoppable
  3. Inner Sight or Aspect of the Umbral on rings - pick whichever your Energy problem actually needs
  4. Remaining points into crit and Poison passives

Rotation

Positioning is the build. Fight with a wall behind or beside the pack so Eaglehorn's arrow bounces back through it for a second hit - Maxroll lists 'requires good positioning' as the build's stated downside, and at push tiers that is where the clear time is won or lost. Shadow Clone on cooldown, Poison Imbuement maintained by attacking, Dash out of danger rather than through it.

Skill point priority

  1. Everything maxed on Penetrating Shot, Poison Imbuement, Shadow Clone
  2. Concealment held for the Unstoppable more than for the Ferocity at this tier
  3. Defensive passives over the last marginal damage nodes

How to run them

The Poison variant hires Subo and takes Incendiary Bolt, Scorched Earth, Snipe and Molotov. Scorched Earth is the reason: it makes enemies take roughly 30% more damage-over-time from you, and damage-over-time is what a Poison Imbuement build is. Reinforce with Varyana on Bloodthirst, with Penetrating Shot as the Opportunity trigger - the Rogue planner stores a real named trigger here, unlike the Druid's. Note the fork with the Cold variant, which hires Raheir instead. Icy Veins disagrees with both and pairs Subo with a Raheir/Bastion reinforcement off Dark Shroud; that is a genuine disagreement rather than a stage difference, and Bastion's 90% damage-reduction bubble is a fair trade if you are dying to burst. Remember a Reinforcement only casts the base version of its skill and gets nothing from its own passives.

Plan

Honest framing first, because it is the whole story here: **no Rogue build guide publishes War Plan picks.** The field exists on all eleven of Maxroll's Penetrating Shot planner profiles and every one of them is empty - seven activity trees, zero nodes selected - and neither Maxroll's nor Icy Veins' guide has a War Plans section at all. That is an authoring gap at the source, not something this site can research away. The system itself is real and worth using: at Tyrael's table in Temis you chain up to five endgame activities, each activity carries its own seven-level tree, and those trees only earn experience while the activity is part of an active plan. The picks below are therefore Maxroll's build-agnostic Season 14 progression priorities, ordered for what this build actually needs - not a Rogue-specific plan anyone published.

Summary

The consensus Rogue meta build. One charged shot pierces an entire enemy line; with Eaglehorn the arrow bounces off walls and returns, so good angles hit the pack twice. Cross-checked across four sources: the freshest patch-3.1.2 class snapshot says Poison Penetrating Shot now leads the class, Maxroll's push list rates it S, Icy Veins' build list rates it S, and Maxroll's week-1 review reports Poison Penetrating Shot leading Tower leaderboards. Disagreement noted and not hidden: Maxroll's *overall endgame* list rates it only A tier, along with every other Rogue build - it has no Rogue build in S. The honest reading is that this build is exceptional at pushing and leaderboard play and merely very good at casual farming, and that it demands positioning discipline that a spin-and-win build does not.
