How to run them

A real divergence from the base build, and one that is easy to miss: the Cold variant hires Raheir where the Poison variant hires Subo. Every Cold profile in the planner takes Raheir with Ground Slam, Bastion, Inspiration and Raheir's Aegis. The logic holds up - Subo's Scorched Earth boosts damage-over-time, which is worth a great deal to Poison Imbuement and close to nothing to Cold, so the Cold build spends the slot on Valiance's cheat-death and Inspiration's damage multiplier instead. The reinforcement is unchanged: Varyana on Bloodthirst, triggered by Penetrating Shot. Icy Veins runs Subo hired for both variants with a Raheir/Bastion reinforcement; that disagreement is worth knowing but the planner is variant-specific and this is a variant question.

Plan

Honest framing first, because it is the whole story here: **no Rogue build guide publishes War Plan picks.** The field exists on all eleven of Maxroll's Penetrating Shot planner profiles and every one of them is empty - seven activity trees, zero nodes selected - and neither Maxroll's nor Icy Veins' guide has a War Plans section at all. That is an authoring gap at the source, not something this site can research away. The system itself is real and worth using: at Tyrael's table in Temis you chain up to five endgame activities, each activity carries its own seven-level tree, and those trees only earn experience while the activity is part of an active plan. The picks below are therefore Maxroll's build-agnostic Season 14 progression priorities, ordered for what this build actually needs - not a Rogue-specific plan anyone published. One adjustment for this variant: it is built for lair bosses and Tower floors, so the Lair Boss tree earns its place here in a way it does not on the general-farming base build.

Summary

The same Penetrating Shot engine as the meta build, run on Cold instead of Poison. This is not a downgrade or a budget version - it is the variant Maxroll's guide documents alongside the Poison one, and Maxroll's week-1 review reports that Cold Rain of Arrows and Poison Penetrating Shot both lead Tower leaderboards, i.e. Cold is a leaderboard-proven damage type on this class. The reason to keep both is that the Vulnerable source moves: Poison gets Vulnerable from Shadow Clone, Cold gets it from Cold Imbuement, which frees Shadow Clone to be a pure damage cooldown and makes the rotation less dependent on the Sightless Set reset loop. Take this variant if you are fighting the Corrupted Reaper and other lair bosses often, or if the Sightless Set has not dropped for you.

Reasoning

Based onPoison Penetrating Shot Rogue
Tweaks vs. the base build
  1. Swap Poison Imbuement for Cold Imbuement, and take Vulnerable from Cold Imbuement rather than from Shadow Clone. Reason: this is the documented mechanical difference between the two variants - it decouples your Vulnerable uptime from your Ultimate's cooldown, which is what makes the Cold version less Sightless-Set-dependent.
  2. Swap Concealment for Smoke Grenade. Reason: in the Cold variant, Smoke Grenade's Stealth is what reduces the Ultimate cooldown via the set, so it takes over the utility role Concealment plays in the Poison build.
  3. Run the Narrow Eye Set 3-piece instead of the Sightless Set. Reason: it generates Dark Shroud, which is the Cold variant's mitigation layer, and 3.1.x buffed the related Nilfur's Narrow Eye 5-piece so it no longer requires a targeted enemy - it now hits a random nearby enemy, which suits a build that is freezing and piercing whole lines rather than focusing one target.
  4. Replace Accursed Touch and Aspect of Debilitating Toxins with Nightstalker's Aspect and Aspect of Frozen Memories. Reason: Accursed Touch is the Poison variant's Vulnerable applicator and is redundant once Cold Imbuement does that job; the two Cold aspects are the documented replacements.
  5. Change the amulet and ring elemental affixes from Poison Damage % to Cold Damage %. Reason: the guide specifies matching the amulet's elemental damage to the variant - carrying Poison affixes on a Cold build is dead stats.
  6. Move Azurewrath up the Mythic priority list, above Fists of Fate. Reason: Azurewrath is listed as Cold-variant-only, so on this build it goes from irrelevant to a top pick, while the Poison build should never chase it.
  7. Lean on the Icefall-style freeze interaction when fighting the Corrupted Reaper: bring the boss to a freeze before the Shadow Clone window rather than opening with the Ultimate. Reason: with Vulnerable coming from Cold Imbuement rather than Shadow Clone, the Ultimate is now a pure damage cooldown and should be spent inside your strongest window instead of used to set one up.
