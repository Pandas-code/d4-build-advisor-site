Whirlwind Barbarian

melee channelled AoE

Barbarian meta unchanged melee channelled AoE

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 14

Move on whenHit level 70, then clear Pit Tier 10 to unlock Torment 1. That is the explicit end of the levelling build - respec to the endgame bar at that point.
Move on whenYou have Gohr's Devastating Grips equipped and all five glyphs past their first Legendary Bonus - then start hunting Superior Lair Keys for the Corrupted Reaper.
Move on whenMelted Heart of Selig equipped, Life affixes cleared off gear, and glyphs levelled evenly - then switch from farming to Pit and Tower pushing.

Rotation

Ground Stomp into the pack to stun and dump Fury, War Cry and Challenging Shout on cooldown, then hold Whirlwind. Lunging Strike whenever Fury runs dry. Call of the Ancients on every elite pack.

Skill point priority

  1. Whirlwind to max first - it becomes your only damage source once Dust Devils unlock at level 15
  2. War Cry next for the damage multiplier and Berserking
  3. Lunging Strike for resource generation and Berserking uptime
  4. Challenging Shout for the enemy debuff
  5. Ground Stomp for crowd control and burst Fury
  6. Call of the Ancients is the levelling ultimate: lowest cooldown and biggest multiplier of the options

Rotation

Always be spinning and moving in circles with Whirlwind. War Cry, Challenging Shout and Wrath of the Berserker on cooldown for close to 100% uptime. Cast Rallying Cry as the previous buff expires - do not spam it, over-casting starves Fury. Lunging Strike only when the resource bottoms out.

Skill point priority

  1. Whirlwind max, with the Tornado upgrade
  2. War Cry max - this is your Vulnerable source and it pairs with the Exploit glyph
  3. Rallying Cry for Fortify and resource
  4. Challenging Shout for survivability
  5. Wrath of the Berserker - now a flat +30% all damage for its duration in 3.1.x, so it is a damage cooldown, not just an Unstoppable button
  6. Warbringer passive for Fortify generation

Rotation

Unchanged core loop. Once Melted Heart of Selig is on, strip every Life affix off your gear - the Mythic doubles your resource pool and converts it into your effective life pool, so Life rolls actively dilute it. Mercenary: Raheir hired, Aldkin's Field of Languish as reinforcement.

Skill point priority

  2. War Cry max for guaranteed Vulnerable

  4. Rallying Cry and Challenging Shout at functional ranks
  5. Ignore Aspect of Limitless Rage entirely: 3.1.x cut it from 1-1.5% to 0.15-0.20% damage per Fury

Rotation

Pull a large group, land War Cry for Vulnerable, pop Wrath of the Berserker for the +30% window, then spin. Keep the ultimate's uptime as close to permanent as your cooldown reduction allows - that window is the whole build now. Avoid 2-Handed Techniques: they are partially or entirely non-functional this patch.

Skill point priority

  1. Whirlwind and War Cry stay maxed
  2. Iron Skin replaces Lunging Strike - at push tiers you die to a single mistake more often than you run dry on Fury
  3. Every remaining point into Wrath of the Berserker uptime and defensive passives
  4. Do not slot Aspect of Walloping: it does not work with the Whirlwind Tornado upgrade

How to run them

Hire Raheir and take Ground Slam, Bastion, Inspiration and Raheir's Aegis. Maxroll's reasoning is that he is "great defensively while at the same time providing a decent damage multiplier thanks to Inspiration", and his Valiance perk is a rotating cheat-death that negates any hit worth 15% or more of your current Life. Reinforce with Aldkin and pick Field of Languish for its area damage reduction. Sources disagree here and it is worth knowing why: Icy Veins reinforces with Varyana instead, taking Bloodthirst off a War Cry trigger for attack speed. That is a straight defence-versus-offence trade, not a right-and-wrong - take Varyana if you are comfortably surviving your content. Remember a Reinforcement only ever casts the base version of its skill and gets nothing from its own passives.

Plan

War Plans is the Lord of Hatred endgame playlist: at the table beside Tyrael in Temis you chain up to five endgame activities, and each of the seven activities carries its own seven-level tree that only earns experience while it is part of an active plan. Boards are per-character and you get three redraws from the "New Plans" button. Be aware of what this list is: Maxroll ships one generic "Default War Plans Tree" shared by every Season 14 Whirlwind profile and says outright that you should not stick to it - "you should adapt yours depending on the content you like to do and or depending on the Materials you need". There is no Whirlwind-specific War Plan. The picks below are the nodes from that default tree that actually matter to this build's gearing path, which runs through Lair Bosses for Gohr's Devastating Grips and the Corrupted Reaper.

Allocation picks9 picks

  • 01Grigoire's Gambit

    Lair Bosses. Grigoire is the dedicated source of Gohr's Devastating Grips, this build's single most important unique - so the Lair Boss tree is where a Whirlwind Barbarian should spend first.

  • 02Two by Two

    Lair Bosses. Two Initiate-tier bosses per Nemesis Lair, which roughly halves the key cost of farming gloves.

  • 03Writhe and Rot

    Helltide. Hellwyrm hunting for experience and Lair Keys - the keys feed the Lair Boss tree above.

  • 04Hellmouth

    Helltide. Opens the Hellwyrm route that Writhe and Rot then pays out on.

  • 05Nemesis

    Nightmare Dungeons. Shrines summon up to seven Elites, which is exactly the pull size Whirlwind wants.

  • 06Heart of Stone

    The Pit. Your glyphs come from Pit runs and nowhere else.

  • 07Choron's Blessing

    The Pit. An extra glyph-upgrade chance per completion - the single biggest lever on how fast Twister, Exploit, Wrath, Challenger and Marshal come online.

  • 08Method and Madness

    Infernal Hordes. Bartuc is the dedicated drop for Tuskhelm of Joritz the Mighty, so the Hordes tree is not optional for this build.

  • 09Resplendent Favor

    Tree of Whispers. A chance at a Resplendent Spark per cache, which feeds the Jeweler route to Melted Heart of Selig.

Summary

The safest pick in Season 14. Whirlwind is the only build rated top-tier by every source consulted: S on Maxroll's overall endgame list, S on Maxroll's push list, S on Icy Veins' build list, and named the best all-around build by the freshest patch-3.1.2 class snapshot. You spin continuously, keep three shouts and Wrath of the Berserker on cooldown, and let Dust Devils do the clearing. It survived the 3.1.0 Fury-generation cap and the Aspect of Limitless Rage gutting because its damage comes from Gohr's Devastating Grips and Aspect of Channeling, not from Fury scaling. Cross-check note: no source disagreed on this build's standing — the only Barbarian caveat anywhere is Icy Veins rating the class B tier for levelling, which is why the levelling stage below leans on Call of the Ancients rather than trying to force the endgame pattern early.
