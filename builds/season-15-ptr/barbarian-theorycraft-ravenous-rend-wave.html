Move on whenLevel 70, Torment 1, and Ring of the Ravenous in hand. Run the stack-counting test in test_notes the moment you equip it, before investing further.

  5. Leap for gap closing
  6. Wrath of the Berserker as the ultimate for its flat +30% all damage

Shouts up, Lunging Strike to sustain Fury, Rend continuously rather than in bursts - the whole point is application frequency.   3. War Cry and Challenging Shout maxed
  4. Rallying Cry for Fortify and Unstoppable only; its Madawc variant was halved 200% to 100% on the PTR, so do not rank it for damage
  5. Wrath of the Berserker as the ultimate

Summary

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; this is untested theorycraft resting on 3.2.0 PTR values that may be revised. It is deliberately a contrarian build. Every source says Barbarian bleed was nerfed in 3.2.0, and they are right about the headline: Two-Handed Sword Expertise's bleeding bonus was halved from 30%[x] to 15%[x] and the Hemorrhage node went 75%[x] to 60%[x]. But Ring of the Ravenous was not simply nerfed, it was reworked - its duration and damage came down (6-8s to 4-6s, 60-80% to 40-60%) while it gained the ability to apply a Rend stack and had its cooldown halved from 1 second to 0.5. A shorter, weaker, twice-as-frequent effect that also applies stacks is a different item, not a worse one. The hypothesis is that Rend-stack application rate now matters more than per-stack magnitude, which would make a fast-hitting Rend build better than the raw numbers suggest. Honest framing: no source supports this, the consensus reading is that Barbarian bleed is dead, and this build exists to test whether the consensus read the rework as a nerf without checking the cooldown line.

Reasoning

HypothesisRing of the Ravenous' halved cooldown (1s to 0.5s) plus its new Rend-stack application means total Rend uptime and stack count roughly double, which more than compensates for the 25-33% cut to its own duration and damage. If Rend damage scales with stack count, a build built for application rate beats one built for per-hit magnitude even after the Two-Handed Sword Expertise bleeding cut from 30%[x] to 15%[x].
Confidencelow
How to test itConfidence is low deliberately - this contradicts every source read. Test cheaply and be willing to abandon it. (1) On a dummy, count Rend stacks applied per ten seconds with Ring of the Ravenous equipped versus not. If the halved cooldown does not translate into roughly double the application rate, the premise is wrong at step one. (2) Confirm whether Rend damage actually scales per stack or whether stacks only refresh a duration. This is the load-bearing unknown and the patch notes do not answer it - if stacks merely refresh, the whole build collapses and should be abandoned without further testing. (3) Compare total damage against barbarian-meta-upheaval-red-furor at equal gear. The meta build's Ring of Red Furor occupies the same ring slot conceptually, so this is a genuine either-or, not a complement. (4) Check whether the Scorching upgrade's new fire wave scales with Rend stacks or is a flat effect - the aggregators do not mention it at all, so this may be entirely unexplored ground or entirely worthless. (5) Watch survivability: a bleed build fights at melee range with a class that just lost 27% of its baseline scaling.
