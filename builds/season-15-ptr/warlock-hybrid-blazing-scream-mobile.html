Build Notes (Shared for all Variants)

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; built on 3.2.0 PTR values that Blizzard may revise. This is warlock-meta-blazing-scream rebuilt around the single quality-of-life theme running through the whole Warlock section of the 3.2.0 notes: Hellfire Aura, Dark Prison's Chain Aura and Bombardment's Endless Barrage all became castable while moving. Season 14 Warlock was a stand-still class with a ramp; three lines in one patch removed the stand-still half. This variant leans all the way into that by swapping the meta build's static trail-painting for a Sigil-heavy bar that keeps damage going out while you never stop, and by taking Sigil of Chaos so Dark Prison's Calamity - which is now also a Sigil Skill at 200% damage, up from 30% - is part of a coherent Sigil package rather than an isolated nuke. It trades some peak single-target for far better uptime in Challenge Dungeons and Overland Ambushes, the two new PTR activities that specifically punish standing still.

Rotation
  1. Keep Hellfire Aura maintained, open every pack with Dark Prison, Bombardment continuously while you walk, and Blazing Scream through the middle.
  2. The whole point is never losing a cast to repositioning, so keep moving.
Variants

Leveling — Keep moving. Hellfire Aura up while you walk, Blazing Scream across packs as you pass through them, Dread Claws on anything that survives. Wall of Agony only when something forces you to hold ground. The habit to build now is never stopping to cast - that is the whole point of the variant.
Move on when Level 70, Torment 1, and Elegy in hand - the same gate as the base build, because it is the same damage spine.

Early Endgame — Hellfire Aura on, then never stop walking. Dark Prison (Calamity) into the pack for the 200% hit, Bombardment while repositioning, Blazing Scream on the pass-through. Sigil of Smoke makes things Vulnerable on hit, which turns on Bane Link Aspect's +15% - so the Vulnerable application is now a damage step, not a debuff you can skip.
Move on when Infernal Homunculus equipped. Free Lesser Demon Upgrades at 200% duration matter more here than anywhere else, because a build that never stands still has no window to re-apply anything by hand.

Late Endgame — Full mobile loop: Hellfire Aura maintained, Dark Prison (Calamity) as the opener into every pack, Bombardment continuously while walking, Blazing Scream through the middle. Overwhelming Aspect's 7-17% per Overpower stack doubles against Hexed enemies, so Hex uptime is still a damage stat here even though the bar is different.
Move on when You are comfortably clearing Challenge Dungeons at one to two Torment tiers above your normal difficulty. That activity is the reason this variant exists - it scales up to three tiers above and does not let you stand still.

Pushing — Accept the trade honestly: this variant gives up the base build's peak burst - the Eightfold Idol trail stacking on an anchored pack - in exchange for never losing a cast to repositioning. On corridor pushes where you are always moving, that is a gain; on a stationary boss, warlock-meta-blazing-scream is the better build and you should swap to it. Everything here describes 3.2.0 PTR behaviour and may change before Season 15 launches.

Build Notes (Shared for all Variants)

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; built on 3.2.0 PTR values that Blizzard may revise. This is warlock-meta-blazing-scream rebuilt around the single quality-of-life theme running through the whole Warlock section of the 3.2.0 notes: Hellfire Aura, Dark Prison's Chain Aura and Bombardment's Endless Barrage all became castable while moving. Season 14 Warlock was a stand-still class with a ramp; three lines in one patch removed the stand-still half. This variant leans all the way into that by swapping the meta build's static trail-painting for a Sigil-heavy bar that keeps damage going out while you never stop, and by taking Sigil of Chaos so Dark Prison's Calamity - which is now also a Sigil Skill at 200% damage, up from 30% - is part of a coherent Sigil package rather than an isolated nuke. It trades some peak single-target for far better uptime in Challenge Dungeons and Overland Ambushes, the two new PTR activities that specifically punish standing still.

Rotation
  1. Keep Hellfire Aura maintained, open every pack with Dark Prison, Bombardment continuously while you walk, and Blazing Scream through the middle.
  2. The whole point is never losing a cast to repositioning, so keep moving.
Variants

Leveling — Keep moving. Hellfire Aura up while you walk, Blazing Scream across packs as you pass through them, Dread Claws on anything that survives. Wall of Agony only when something forces you to hold ground. The habit to build now is never stopping to cast - that is the whole point of the variant.
Move on when Level 70, Torment 1, and Elegy in hand - the same gate as the base build, because it is the same damage spine.

Early Endgame — Hellfire Aura on, then never stop walking. Dark Prison (Calamity) into the pack for the 200% hit, Bombardment while repositioning, Blazing Scream on the pass-through. Sigil of Smoke makes things Vulnerable on hit, which turns on Bane Link Aspect's +15% - so the Vulnerable application is now a damage step, not a debuff you can skip.
Move on when Infernal Homunculus equipped. Free Lesser Demon Upgrades at 200% duration matter more here than anywhere else, because a build that never stands still has no window to re-apply anything by hand.

Late Endgame — Full mobile loop: Hellfire Aura maintained, Dark Prison (Calamity) as the opener into every pack, Bombardment continuously while walking, Blazing Scream through the middle. Overwhelming Aspect's 7-17% per Overpower stack doubles against Hexed enemies, so Hex uptime is still a damage stat here even though the bar is different.
Move on when You are comfortably clearing Challenge Dungeons at one to two Torment tiers above your normal difficulty. That activity is the reason this variant exists - it scales up to three tiers above and does not let you stand still.

Pushing — Accept the trade honestly: this variant gives up the base build's peak burst - the Eightfold Idol trail stacking on an anchored pack - in exchange for never losing a cast to repositioning. On corridor pushes where you are always moving, that is a gain; on a stationary boss, warlock-meta-blazing-scream is the better build and you should swap to it. Reasoning

Tweaks vs. the base build
  1. Bar swap: Umbral Chains out, Sigil of Chaos in. The meta build uses Umbral Chains to pull packs to a fixed spot so the Eightfold Idol trails land where enemies will be; this variant never stands at a fixed spot, so the pull is dead weight. Sigil of Chaos brings Hellfire Aura (castable while moving on the PTR) and Sigil of Smoke (makes enemies Vulnerable on hit), which feeds Bane Link Aspect's +15% damage to Vulnerable enemies.
  2. Dark Prison is played for Calamity rather than for Chain Aura's control. Calamity went 30% to 200% damage on the PTR and became a Sigil Skill, so on this bar it counts for both the damage and the Sigil package - the meta build treats it as a lock-down tool instead.
  3. Aspect swap: Enfeebling Aspect out, Misfortune Aspect in on the amulet. Enfeebling's Weaken rider matters when you hold a pack in place; Misfortune's flat 15% reduction from Hexed enemies is unconditional and this variant takes more incidental hits because it fights while repositioning.
  4. Aspect swap: Nefarious Aspect out, Cauterization Aspect in on the boots. Nefarious converts direct damage to Shadow while in Shadowform, which pairs with the meta build's trail stacking; Cauterization converts to Fire while Volatile and adds a 40% chance to Fortify 3-6% Maximum Life, which is the survivability this variant needs when it is inside packs rather than behind trails.
  5. Paragon reweighting: Control glyph is taken for its 15% against Slowed/Taunted rather than its 30% against Tethered/Knocked Down, because dropping Umbral Chains removes the Tether source. This is a real downgrade and is accepted deliberately - do not take Control glyph at all if you are short on glyph slots.
  6. Soul Splinter unchanged: Splinter of Anguish, not Hellfire. Both builds scale with critical strikes, so Hellfire's -100% Critical Strike Chance is disqualifying for both. Anguish's +30% Primary Resource cost bites harder here because this variant casts more often - budget more Dominance regeneration on boots and helm to pay for it.
  7. Gear: The Eightfold Idol stays but drops in priority. Its Shadow and Lava trails are worth less to a build that does not control where enemies walk. If you have not found it, this variant is playable without it, which the meta build genuinely is not.
