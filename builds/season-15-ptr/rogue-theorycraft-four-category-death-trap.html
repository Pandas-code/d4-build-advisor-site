Four-Category Death Trap Rogue

multi-category trap hybrid

Rogue theorycraft unchanged multi-category trap hybrid confidence medium PTR

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 15 (PTR)

Move on whenLevel 70, Torment 1, and enough paragon access to actually reach Tricks of the Trade. Until you can take that node this build has no reason to exist.
Move on whenThe dummy test has passed and the four multipliers demonstrably stack. If it failed, the correct next step is to play rogue-meta-penetrating-shot instead and record the negative result.
Move on whenClear times measurably beat rogue-meta-penetrating-shot at equal gear. If they merely match it, this build is a curiosity rather than an upgrade and should be reported as such.

Skill bar

1Forceful Arrow
2Penetrating Shot
3Dash
4Twisting Blades
5Poison Imbuement
6Death Trap

Rotation

Play it like the meta build: Poison Imbuement up, Forceful Arrow to refill, Penetrating Shot down lines, Dash between packs, Death Trap on elites. Use the time to notice whether Twisting Blades and Penetrating Shot on one bar feels workable at all.

Skill point priority

  1. Penetrating Shot as the primary damage skill while levelling - the four-category plan is a paragon-era idea and the node does not exist yet
  2. Forceful Arrow for Energy; running four actives later makes early Energy habits matter more, not less
  3. Twisting Blades kept at low rank purely to learn whether it feels usable alongside a bow skill - it was buffed 30%/70% to 42%/98% in 3.1.x and is not dead weight
  4. Dash for mobility
  5. Poison Imbuement for its attacking-maintains-uptime loop
  6. Death Trap as the ultimate rather than Shadow Clone - it is the Trap-category anchor the whole build is planned around

Rotation

Death Trap into the pack, Smoke Grenade on top of it, Dash in, Twisting Blades on whatever is clumped, Penetrating Shot down the line as you back out. Poison Imbuement maintained by attacking. The rotation is deliberately busier than a conventional Rogue's - that is the cost being tested.

Skill point priority

  1. Split rather than concentrate - that is the experiment. Penetrating Shot and Twisting Blades share the damage load instead of one carrying it
  2. Smoke Grenade for the Trap category alongside Death Trap, and because its Shade Grenade variant now gains Combo Point damage on the PTR
  3. Dash for the Mobility category and Ferocity stacking
  4. Poison Imbuement to convert both damage skills at once - Aspect of Imitated Imbuement is what makes a split bar affordable
  5. Death Trap as the ultimate and the Trap anchor
  6. Inner Sight specialization for Energy, which is under more pressure here than on any other Rogue build

  4. Poison Imbuement maxed

How to run them

[ptr] 3.2.0 changes nothing about mercenaries - no mercenary name appears anywhere in the official 3.2.0 notes. This is the Season 14 Poison Penetrating Shot pairing carried over, which is the only guidance that exists. Hire Subo and take Incendiary Bolt, Scorched Earth, Snipe and Molotov; Scorched Earth's roughly 30% increase to your damage-over-time is what makes him the Poison variant's pick over Raheir, who the Cold variant hires instead. Reinforce with Varyana on Bloodthirst, Opportunity trigger Penetrating Shot. Icy Veins reinforces with Raheir/Bastion off Dark Shroud instead; that disagreement predates the PTR and 3.2.0 does nothing to settle it. Bloodthirst's attack speed is worth more on this build than on the base one, because a four-skill rotation lives or dies on how quickly you can cycle it.

Summary

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; this is untested theorycraft built on a 3.2.0 PTR node whose exact wording may change. The new Tricks of the Trade paragon node grants 20%[x] increased damage for Marksman, Cutthroat, Mobility and Trap skills - each. Every existing Rogue build is built the opposite way, stacking one category and ignoring the rest, because until this patch there was no reward for breadth. This build deliberately gives up specialisation to bank all four multipliers: Penetrating Shot for Marksman, Twisting Blades for Cutthroat, Dash for Mobility, Death Trap for Trap. If the four multipliers are genuinely independent and multiplicative, the stacked total is roughly 2.07x against a mono-category bar's 1.2x, which would be worth far more than the damage lost by splitting skill points. That is the bet, and it is a bet - no source recommends this and the node's exact stacking behaviour is not documented anywhere outside the patch notes line itself.

Reasoning

HypothesisTricks of the Trade's four category bonuses stack multiplicatively and independently rather than being a single 20%[x] that any qualifying skill switches on. If so, a bar covering Marksman, Cutthroat, Mobility and Trap earns about 2.07x from the node where a conventional single-category bar earns 1.2x, and that gap is larger than the damage lost by spreading skill points across four skills instead of concentrating them on one core skill.
Confidencemedium
How to test itThe whole build lives or dies on one question, so test it first and cheaply. (1) On a training dummy, record damage with only Penetrating Shot on the bar, then add Dash, then Smoke Grenade, then Twisting Blades, recording after each. If damage steps up roughly 20% multiplicatively at each addition, the hypothesis holds. If it steps once and then flatlines, the node is a single flat bonus with four triggers and this build is strictly worse than rogue-meta-penetrating-shot - abandon it immediately, do not rationalise. (2) Confirm which category each skill actually counts as in game; the patch notes name the four categories but do not enumerate skill membership, and the assignments in this build are inference from skill behaviour, not from a source. Death Trap counting as Trap and Dash as Mobility are safe; Twisting Blades counting as Cutthroat is the assumption most likely to be wrong. (3) Compare Pit clear time against rogue-meta-penetrating-shot at equal gear and paragon. (4) Watch Energy: four active skills on an already Energy-starved class is the second most likely failure mode after the stacking question.
