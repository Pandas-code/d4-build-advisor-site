Build Notes (Shared for all Variants)

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; every number here comes from the 3.2.0 PTR build (#73123, 2026-08-04 to 2026-08-11) and Blizzard routinely revises PTR values before release. This is the strongest emerging PTR consensus build. The unique Elegy turns Blazing Scream into an Archfiend Skill with 30-50% increased damage, hands you Skull Splitter for free and makes the skulls match its main damage; The Eightfold Idol then makes every Blazing Scream leave Shadow and Lava trails carrying 60-80% increased Abyss and Hellfire damage, so the skill stops being a burst and becomes a persistent damage field. Infernal Homunculus adds 80-110% to Archfiend skills, which Elegy has just made Blazing Scream one of. Both PTR class tier lists found rate Warlock S, and both specifically name Blazing Scream (Elegy rework) as the class's headline build. Honest counterweight, and it is a real one: Blizzard also gave Warlock the largest buff pass at patch 3.1.0 and the class still finished Season 14 dead last on the freshest class snapshot - knowledge/classes/warlock.md records that as the season's widest source disagreement. No tier-consensus source (Maxroll, Icy Veins, TierListHQ) has published a 3.2.0 list yet, so the S placement rests on two low-authority aggregators plus the size of the official numbers, not on measured Pit results.

Rotation
  1. Paint first, then push.
  2. Lay Blazing Scream across the pack's approach line so The Eightfold Idol's trails are already down when they arrive, Dark Prison to hold them on the trails, Bombardment while moving, Apocalypse on cooldown.
Variants

Leveling — Umbral Chains to pull a pack together, Blazing Scream through the middle of it, Dread Claws on anything still standing. Wall of Agony between you and ranged attackers. Apocalypse on elites. Do not try to manage both resources yet - just keep Wrath spending below what Blazing Scream needs.
Move on when Level 70 with Torment 1 unlocked, and Elegy in hand. Without Elegy this is not the same build - Blazing Scream is not an Archfiend Skill and Infernal Homunculus does nothing for it.

Early Endgame — Umbral Chains to group, Dark Prison (Calamity) into the pack, then Blazing Scream through it. Bombardment now goes out while you reposition rather than standing still - Endless Barrage is castable while moving on the PTR, so the correct habit is to never stop. Dread Claws maintained on elites. Apocalypse for the damage window.
Move on when The Eightfold Idol equipped. The moment Blazing Scream starts leaving trails the build changes character - you stop aiming at enemies and start painting the ground they have to walk across.

Late Endgame — Paint first, then push. Blazing Scream across the pack's approach line so The Eightfold Idol's Shadow and Lava trails are already down when they arrive, then Dark Prison (Calamity) to hold them on the trails. Bombardment goes out while moving. Overwhelming Aspect pays 7-17% per Overpower stack and doubles it against Hexed targets, so Hex uptime is a damage stat, not a utility one. Apocalypse on cooldown.
Move on when Infernal Homunculus equipped and Superiority ranked. Its free Lesser Demon Upgrades at 200% duration remove the ramp, which is the thing that stopped Warlock pushing in Season 14.

Pushing — The Season 15 PTR Warlock pushes by pre-placing, not by reacting. Lay Blazing Scream trails on the corridor before you enter it, Dark Prison to lock the pack onto the trails, Bombardment while walking backwards along them. Misanthropic Aspect gives 25-45% Damage Reduction only when at most one Demon has been alive for more than two seconds, so on a demon-light push bar it is close to permanent - check that before relying on it. Everything here is PTR-build behaviour and may not survive to launch.

Uniques
  • weapon PTR route: take PTR Boosts from the vendor Mrak, who stands next to the main Waypoint in every major city on the 3.2.0 PTR - his boosts include level 70, maxed aspects, random legendaries and uniques, and seasonal items, which is how PTR testers get a specific unique in minutes rather than farming for it. Live route once Season 15 ships: no boss loot table has been published for 3.2.0, so expect either a targeted lair-boss farm or the Horadric Cube 'Upgrade to Unique' recipe as in Season 14 - re-verify against the launch patch notes before relying on it.   • offhand PTR route: Mrak's PTR Boosts (random uniques) next to the main Waypoint in any major city. Live route: unpublished for 3.2.0; re-verify at launch. Do not equip it before Elegy - the trails only matter once Blazing Scream is an Archfiend Skill. Where →
  • chest PTR route: Mrak's PTR Boosts. Live route: unpublished for 3.2.0. Its second line (Lesser Demon Upgrades activate free with 200% longer durations) is worth as much as the 80-110% Archfiend damage, because it removes the ramp cost the class is known for. Where →
  • ring PTR route: Mrak's PTR Boosts. Live route: unpublished for 3.2.0. Buffed to a 100-130% damage bonus on the PTR. Where →
  • amulet PTR route: Mrak's PTR Boosts. Live route: unpublished for 3.2.0. Buffed to a 120-130% damage bonus on the PTR. Where →
  • helm Mythic Unique, all classes. PTR route: Mrak's PTR Boosts. Live route (as of Season 14, and 3.2.0 keeps all three Mythic paths): no dedicated boss - craft it at the Jeweler from Resplendent Sparks and Runes choosing the helm slot, which on the PTR carries no Crafted tag so it does not consume your one crafted-Mythic slot; or take it from a Blacksmith Mythic cache; or drop it from any boss.   • SSplinter of Anguish jewelry socket New Soul Splinter socketable, jewelry only. PTR route: Mrak's PTR Boosts explicitly include seasonal items such as Soul Splinters. Live route: unpublished. Take Anguish rather than Splinter of Hellfire on this build - Hellfire's 40-100% damage costs 100% Critical Strike Chance, which deletes the crit scaling this build's paragon and gear are built on, while Anguish buys 15-35% Critical Strike Chance and Attack Speed for a 30% Primary Resource cost increase you can pay with Dominance regeneration. PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; every number here comes from the 3.2.0 PTR build (#73123, 2026-08-04 to 2026-08-11) and Blizzard routinely revises PTR values before release. This is the strongest emerging PTR consensus build. The unique Elegy turns Blazing Scream into an Archfiend Skill with 30-50% increased damage, hands you Skull Splitter for free and makes the skulls match its main damage; The Eightfold Idol then makes every Blazing Scream leave Shadow and Lava trails carrying 60-80% increased Abyss and Hellfire damage, so the skill stops being a burst and becomes a persistent damage field. Infernal Homunculus adds 80-110% to Archfiend skills, which Elegy has just made Blazing Scream one of. Both PTR class tier lists found rate Warlock S, and both specifically name Blazing Scream (Elegy rework) as the class's headline build. Honest counterweight, and it is a real one: Blizzard also gave Warlock the largest buff pass at patch 3.1.0 and the class still finished Season 14 dead last on the freshest class snapshot - knowledge/classes/warlock.md records that as the season's widest source disagreement. No tier-consensus source (Maxroll, Icy Veins, TierListHQ) has published a 3.2.0 list yet, so the S placement rests on two low-authority aggregators plus the size of the official numbers, not on measured Pit results.

Rotation
  1. Paint first, then push.
  2. Lay Blazing Scream across the pack's approach line so The Eightfold Idol's trails are already down when they arrive, Dark Prison to hold them on the trails, Bombardment while moving, Apocalypse on cooldown.
Variants

Leveling — Umbral Chains to pull a pack together, Blazing Scream through the middle of it, Dread Claws on anything still standing. Wall of Agony between you and ranged attackers. Apocalypse on elites. Do not try to manage both resources yet - just keep Wrath spending below what Blazing Scream needs.
Move on when Level 70 with Torment 1 unlocked, and Elegy in hand. Without Elegy this is not the same build - Blazing Scream is not an Archfiend Skill and Infernal Homunculus does nothing for it.

Early Endgame — Umbral Chains to group, Dark Prison (Calamity) into the pack, then Blazing Scream through it. Bombardment now goes out while you reposition rather than standing still - Endless Barrage is castable while moving on the PTR, so the correct habit is to never stop. Dread Claws maintained on elites. Apocalypse for the damage window.
Move on when The Eightfold Idol equipped. The moment Blazing Scream starts leaving trails the build changes character - you stop aiming at enemies and start painting the ground they have to walk across.

Late Endgame — Paint first, then push. Blazing Scream across the pack's approach line so The Eightfold Idol's Shadow and Lava trails are already down when they arrive, then Dark Prison (Calamity) to hold them on the trails. Bombardment goes out while moving. Overwhelming Aspect pays 7-17% per Overpower stack and doubles it against Hexed targets, so Hex uptime is a damage stat, not a utility one. Apocalypse on cooldown.
Move on when Infernal Homunculus equipped and Superiority ranked. Its free Lesser Demon Upgrades at 200% duration remove the ramp, which is the thing that stopped Warlock pushing in Season 14.

Pushing — The Season 15 PTR Warlock pushes by pre-placing, not by reacting. Lay Blazing Scream trails on the corridor before you enter it, Dark Prison to lock the pack onto the trails, Bombardment while walking backwards along them. Misanthropic Aspect gives 25-45% Damage Reduction only when at most one Demon has been alive for more than two seconds, so on a demon-light push bar it is close to permanent - check that before relying on it. Everything here is PTR-build behaviour and may not survive to launch.
