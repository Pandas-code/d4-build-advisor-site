Elegy Blazing Scream Warlock

archfiend caster

Warlock meta unchanged archfiend caster PTR

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 15 (PTR)

Move on whenLevel 70 with Torment 1 unlocked, and Elegy in hand. Without Elegy this is not the same build - Blazing Scream is not an Archfiend Skill and Infernal Homunculus does nothing for it.
Move on whenThe Eightfold Idol equipped. The moment Blazing Scream starts leaving trails the build changes character - you stop aiming at enemies and start painting the ground they have to walk across.
Move on whenInfernal Homunculus equipped and Superiority ranked. Its free Lesser Demon Upgrades at 200% duration remove the ramp, which is the thing that stopped Warlock pushing in Season 14.

Rotation

Umbral Chains to pull a pack together, Blazing Scream through the middle of it, Dread Claws on anything still standing. Wall of Agony between you and ranged attackers. Apocalypse on elites. Do not try to manage both resources yet - just keep Wrath spending below what Blazing Scream needs.

Skill point priority

  1. Blazing Scream to max first - it is the only skill Elegy and The Eightfold Idol care about, and everything later is built on it
  2. Dread Claws next: Ravenous Jaws went from 125% to 500% Eviscerate damage on the PTR, which makes it a real levelling damage skill rather than filler
  3. Umbral Chains for grouping - the 3.1.x fix that stopped it walking forward at out-of-range targets still applies, so it is finally usable at low levels
  4. Wall of Agony (Encircle) for survivability while your Dominance pool is small
  5. Command Fallen (Fallen Rush) to keep bodies on the field between packs
  6. Apocalypse as the ultimate - Warlock's most reliable levelling panic button

Rotation

Umbral Chains to group, Dark Prison (Calamity) into the pack, then Blazing Scream through it. Bombardment now goes out while you reposition rather than standing still - Endless Barrage is castable while moving on the PTR, so the correct habit is to never stop. Dread Claws maintained on elites. Apocalypse for the damage window.

Skill point priority

  1. Blazing Scream maxed - Elegy's 30-50% increased damage multiplies off the ranks, not instead of them
  2. Dark Prison next, taking the Calamity upgrade: 30% to 200% damage on the PTR and Calamity now counts as a Sigil Skill, which matters for anything keyed off Sigil casts
  3. Bombardment with Endless Barrage, which became castable while moving on the PTR - this is the change that makes the bar feel different from Season 14
  4. Dread Claws kept for Ravenous Jaws' 500% Eviscerate damage
  5. Umbral Chains for grouping
  6. Apocalypse as the ultimate

Rotation

Paint first, then push. Blazing Scream across the pack's approach line so The Eightfold Idol's Shadow and Lava trails are already down when they arrive, then Dark Prison (Calamity) to hold them on the trails. Bombardment goes out while moving. Overwhelming Aspect pays 7-17% per Overpower stack and doubles it against Hexed targets, so Hex uptime is a damage stat, not a utility one. Apocalypse on cooldown.

Skill point priority

  1. Blazing Scream, Dark Prison and Bombardment all maxed
  2. Dread Claws held at whatever rank keeps Ravenous Jaws' Eviscerate procs frequent
  3. Umbral Chains kept at low rank - it is a utility pull, not a damage skill
  4. Remaining points into Shadow and Hellfire damage passives, then Dominance regeneration

Rotation

The Season 15 PTR Warlock pushes by pre-placing, not by reacting. Lay Blazing Scream trails on the corridor before you enter it, Dark Prison to lock the pack onto the trails, Bombardment while walking backwards along them. Misanthropic Aspect gives 25-45% Damage Reduction only when at most one Demon has been alive for more than two seconds, so on a demon-light push bar it is close to permanent - check that before relying on it. Everything here is PTR-build behaviour and may not survive to launch.

Skill point priority

  1. Everything maxed on Blazing Scream, Dark Prison and Bombardment
  2. Defensive passives over the last marginal damage nodes - Warlock has no Season 14 reputation for survivability and 3.2.0 did not give it one
  3. Umbral Chains ranked up if you are taking Control glyph's 30% Tethered bonus rather than its 15% Slowed bonus

Board & glyph focus

Not open yet. Bank the points. On the PTR you can skip this stage entirely - Mrak's PTR Boosts grant level 70 and maxed aspects directly - but the stage is written out because it is what the build looks like when Season 15 goes live for real.

Board & glyph focus

Superiority glyph first - it is new in 3.2.0 and does three jobs at once (+30% to magic nodes, +10% Damage Reduction, +2.0% Dominance regeneration), which is exactly the three things a ramping Warlock is short of early. Occultist glyph second (12% to 15% on the PTR). Then damage boards, then Dominance regeneration. Control glyph only if your bar actually applies Slow, Taunt, Tether or Knock Down - its new split is 15% against Slowed/Taunted but 30% against Tethered/Knocked Down, so Umbral Chains changes its value.

Board & glyph focus

Superiority and Occultist maxed, then the magic-node boards Superiority multiplies. Note the gearing rule the PTR changed: the Jeweler's rune-crafted Mythics carry no Crafted tag on 3.2.0, so the one-crafted-Mythic cap that shaped Season 14 gearing no longer binds - you can wear Harlequin Crest from the Jeweler and still craft something else in the Horadric Cube.

Board & glyph focus

Fully min-maxed magic-node routes under Superiority, with enough Dominance regeneration that you never stall mid-pull. Diabolic Armor's 10-15% Damage Reduction and Scorn's flat 15% are cheap at push tiers and 3.2.0 handed both out for free - take them over the last damage node.

Elegyweapon

PTR route: take PTR Boosts from the vendor Mrak, who stands next to the main Waypoint in every major city on the 3.2.0 PTR - his boosts include level 70, maxed aspects, random legendaries and uniques, and seasonal items, which is how PTR testers get a specific unique in minutes rather than farming for it. Live route once Season 15 ships: no boss loot table has been published for 3.2.0, so expect either a targeted lair-boss farm or the Horadric Cube 'Upgrade to Unique' recipe as in Season 14 - re-verify against the launch patch notes before relying on it.

Summary

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; every number here comes from the 3.2.0 PTR build (#73123, 2026-08-04 to 2026-08-11) and Blizzard routinely revises PTR values before release. This is the strongest emerging PTR consensus build. The unique Elegy turns Blazing Scream into an Archfiend Skill with 30-50% increased damage, hands you Skull Splitter for free and makes the skulls match its main damage; The Eightfold Idol then makes every Blazing Scream leave Shadow and Lava trails carrying 60-80% increased Abyss and Hellfire damage, so the skill stops being a burst and becomes a persistent damage field. Infernal Homunculus adds 80-110% to Archfiend skills, which Elegy has just made Blazing Scream one of. Both PTR class tier lists found rate Warlock S, and both specifically name Blazing Scream (Elegy rework) as the class's headline build. Honest counterweight, and it is a real one: Blizzard also gave Warlock the largest buff pass at patch 3.1.0 and the class still finished Season 14 dead last on the freshest class snapshot - knowledge/classes/warlock.md records that as the season's widest source disagreement. No tier-consensus source (Maxroll, Icy Veins, TierListHQ) has published a 3.2.0 list yet, so the S placement rests on two low-authority aggregators plus the size of the official numbers, not on measured Pit results.
