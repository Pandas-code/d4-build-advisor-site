Poison Penetrating Shot Rogue - Grenadier Variant

ranged piercing bow with trap package

Rogue hybrid unchanged ranged piercing bow with trap package based on Poison Penetrating Shot Rogue PTR

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 15 (PTR)

Move on whenLevel 70 with Torment 1 unlocked and Aspect of Imitated Imbuement in hand - the same gate as the base build.
Move on whenThe Sightless Set assembled and Grenadier's Aspect at a usable roll. Both matter - without Grenadier's this is just the base build with a worse talisman.
Move on whenComfortable clears of pack-dense content - Challenge Dungeons and Overland Ambushes are the 3.2.0 activities this variant is tuned for. If your content is boss-heavy, swap to rogue-meta-penetrating-shot instead of pushing this.

Rotation

Smoke Grenade into the pack to crowd-control it, then Penetrating Shot through the line while Grenadier's higher band is active. Poison Imbuement maintained by attacking, Dash between packs, Shadow Clone on elites.

Skill point priority

  1. Penetrating Shot to max - identical to the base build while levelling
  2. Forceful Arrow for Energy
  3. Smoke Grenade earlier than the base build takes it, so you learn its radius and timing before it becomes load-bearing
  4. Poison Imbuement for the attacking-maintains-uptime loop
  5. Dash for mobility. Its Shadow Play variant is renamed Echo on the PTR - same skill
  6. Shadow Clone as the ultimate

Skill point priority

  3. Poison Imbuement max
  4. Shadow Clone max as the Poison variant's Vulnerable source
  5. Dash for Ferocity and the Mobility category
  6. Inner Sight specialization for Energy

Summary

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; built on 3.2.0 PTR values Blizzard may revise. This is rogue-meta-penetrating-shot with the Trap package taken seriously instead of taken incidentally. The meta build carries Smoke Grenade mainly to bank Tricks of the Trade's Trap category; this variant builds around it, because 3.2.0 buffed the entire grenade line at once: Grenadier's Aspect went 35-50%[x] to 45-60%[x] and, crucially, 52.5-75%[x] to 75-100%[x] against Crowd Controlled enemies, while Smoke Grenade's Shade Grenade variant gained Combo Point damage and Shade damage bonuses. Since Smoke Grenade also applies the crowd control that unlocks Grenadier's higher band, the build supplies its own condition - and Applied Alchemy's 5-piece went 200%[x] to 250%[x] on top. The trade is real: less raw single-target than the meta build, more consistent pack damage and better survivability from Aspect of Poisonous Clouds' new 7-10%[x] damage reduction on Poisoned enemies.

Reasoning

Based onPoison Penetrating Shot Rogue
Tweaks vs. the base build
  1. Grenadier's Aspect promoted from unused to core, on gloves. On the PTR it reaches 75-100%[x] against Crowd Controlled enemies, and Smoke Grenade is the crowd control - the build creates its own trigger condition rather than hoping for one.
  2. Smoke Grenade is played on cooldown as a damage skill rather than pressed occasionally for utility. Its Shade Grenade variant now gains Combo Point damage and Shade damage bonuses when using Combo Points, which is a change the meta build does not exploit.
  3. Talisman swap: Nilfur's Narrow Eye out, Applied Alchemy 5-piece in. Narrow Eye's PTR gain is extended Marksman range, which suits the meta build's line-up-the-shot game; Applied Alchemy's 200%[x] to 250%[x] is category-neutral and pays on the grenade damage too.
  4. Aspect swap: Aspect of Alchemical Advantage out, Grenadier's Aspect in on gloves. Alchemical Advantage scales with Poison stacking on a single target; Grenadier's pays on every grenade against every crowd-controlled enemy, which is the shape of this variant's damage.
  5. Aspect of Poisonous Clouds is mandatory here rather than optional. Its new PTR rider makes Poisoned enemies deal 7-10%[x] reduced damage, and this variant spends more time at mid range inside the grenade radius than the meta build does at long range.
  6. Paragon reweighting: Tricks of the Trade is still first, but weight the Trap side of the tree over the Marksman side. The meta build takes the reverse weighting.
  7. Soul Splinter unchanged: Splinter of Anguish, never Splinter of Hellfire - the -100% Critical Strike Chance disqualifies Hellfire for any crit-scaling Rogue. Anguish's +30% Primary Resource cost is more affordable here than on the meta build because grenades cost cooldowns rather than Energy.
  8. Do not take this variant for boss-only content. Grenadier's higher band needs crowd-controlled targets and bosses resist crowd control - against a single boss the meta build is straightforwardly better and you should swap.
