Poison Penetrating Shot Rogue

ranged piercing bow

Rogue meta adjusted ranged piercing bow PTR

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 15 (PTR)

Move on whenLevel 70 with Torment 1 unlocked, and Aspect of Imitated Imbuement in hand.
Move on whenThe Sightless Set assembled - once Shadow Clone starts resetting rapidly the rotation changes character entirely.
Move on whenEaglehorn equipped and the Sightless Set complete - at that point start Pit and Tower pushing rather than farming.

Skill bar

1Forceful Arrow
2Penetrating Shot
3Dash
4Shadow Step
5Poison Imbuement
6Shadow Clone

Rotation

Poison Imbuement up, Forceful Arrow to refill Energy, then Penetrating Shot down the longest line of enemies you can find. Dash between packs. Shadow Clone on every elite.

Skill point priority

  1. Penetrating Shot to max - it is the only damage skill that matters
  2. Forceful Arrow for Energy generation; the build is Energy-hungry from the very start and 3.2.0 does nothing to fix that
  3. Poison Imbuement next - its cooldown shortens as you land hits, so uptime comes from attacking rather than waiting
  4. Dash and Shadow Step for mobility. Note the 3.2.0 rename: Dash's Shadow Play variant is now called Echo. Same skill, new label
  5. Shadow Clone as the ultimate - it applies Vulnerable and Weaken and resets cooldowns

Rotation

Keep Poison Imbuement up by hitting things, not by waiting on the cooldown. Shadow Clone for the damage window and the Vulnerable application. Smoke Grenade into the pack before the shot. Dash between packs to stack Ferocity. Penetrating Shot continuously down enemy lines. Talisman at this stage: Spellbound Steel, which applies Shadow Imbuement while you progress toward the real set.

Skill point priority

  1. Penetrating Shot max
  2. Poison Imbuement max - the Poison variant's damage identity
  3. Shadow Clone max: it is the Poison variant's Vulnerable source, a different role than it plays in the Cold variant
  4. Smoke Grenade replaces Concealment on the PTR bar. Two reasons: it banks the Trap category for Tricks of the Trade's 20%[x], and its Shade Grenade variant now gains Combo Point damage and Shade damage bonuses when using Combo Points
  5. Dash for Ferocity stacks, mobility, and the Mobility category on Tricks of the Trade
  6. Inner Sight specialization to help with Energy

  2. Smoke Grenade kept for the Trap category and the Shade Grenade combo-point damage

  4. Remaining points into crit and Poison passives

Rotation

Positioning is still the build. Fight with a wall behind or beside the pack so Eaglehorn's arrow bounces back through it for a second hit - Maxroll lists 'requires good positioning' as this build's stated downside and at push tiers that is where clear time is won or lost. The PTR's extended Marksman range makes some of those angles easier, not automatic. Shadow Clone on cooldown, Poison Imbuement maintained by attacking, Dash out of danger rather than through it. Everything above describes the 3.2.0 PTR build and may change before Season 15 launches.

Skill point priority

  1. Everything maxed on Penetrating Shot, Poison Imbuement and Shadow Clone
  2. Smoke Grenade held for the Trap category multiplier and the Unstoppable window more than for its own damage
  3. Defensive passives over the last marginal damage nodes

Summary

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; every 3.2.0 number here comes from a one-week PTR build and Blizzard routinely revises them before release. Season 14's Rogue meta build carries forward essentially intact, which is itself the finding: Rogue is the only class near the top that took no meaningful nerf in 3.2.0. Two changes make it better rather than merely unhurt. Nilfur's Narrow Eye's 5-piece now increases Marksman skill range, which is a direct buff to a skill whose entire value is how many enemies one shot can line up. And the new Tricks of the Trade paragon node grants 20%[x] increased damage for each of Marksman, Cutthroat, Mobility and Trap skills on the bar - Penetrating Shot and Forceful Arrow cover Marksman, Dash covers Mobility, and Smoke Grenade covers Trap, so the standard bar already banks three of the four multipliers without changing a thing. Aspect of Poisonous Clouds additionally now makes Poisoned enemies deal 7-10%[x] reduced damage, which is free defence for the Poison variant specifically. Marked adjusted rather than unchanged because the paragon route and one talisman value genuinely move.
