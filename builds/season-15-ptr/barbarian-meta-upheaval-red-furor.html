Ring of Red Furor Upheaval Barbarian

fury-banking two-hander

Barbarian meta adjusted fury-banking two-hander PTR

Last verified 2026-08-09 • Season 15 (PTR)

Move on whenLevel 70 with Torment 1 unlocked, and Ring of Red Furor in hand. Before that ring this is a mediocre post-nerf Barbarian and there is no reason to prefer it.
Move on whenRing of Red Furor equipped with a Maximum Fury roll, and your resting Fury visibly sitting well above 30 between packs rather than bottoming out.
Move on whenResting Fury high enough that Ring of Red Furor's bonus is a visible share of your sheet crit damage. If it is not, the build has not come online yet and pushing will be punishing.

Skill bar

1Lunging Strike
2Upheaval
3War Cry
4Challenging Shout
5Leap
6Wrath of the Berserker

Rotation

Shouts up, Lunging Strike to build Fury, Upheaval on packs. Do not develop the Season 14 habit of dumping Fury the moment you have it - start now with the discipline of holding a high bar, because from the endgame onwards that bar is a damage stat.

Skill point priority

  1. Upheaval to max - it is the core skill this build banks Fury for
  2. Lunging Strike for Fury generation; note that 3.1.x capped Fury generation at 0.5 per 1% Maximum Life and cut it off from Resource Generation bonuses, and 3.2.0 does not undo that
  3. War Cry for Berserking and Vulnerable
  4. Challenging Shout for the damage reduction, which matters more now that the core stat scalar is down
  5. Leap for mobility and gap closing
  6. Wrath of the Berserker as the ultimate - its flat +30% all damage from 3.1.x still applies

Rotation

Shouts to open, Lunging Strike to fill the Fury bar, then Upheaval only when the bar is high - because the bar height is the multiplier. This is the opposite of Season 14 Barbarian rhythm and it takes deliberate unlearning. Raid Leader's Aspect now carries 7-10% Damage Reduction on the PTR, which is free mitigation to offset the scalar cut.

Skill point priority

  1. Upheaval maxed
  2. Lunging Strike maxed for Fury generation - Fury throughput is now damage throughput, not just uptime
  3. War Cry and Challenging Shout maxed for Berserking, Vulnerable and damage reduction
  4. Rallying Cry for Fortify and Unstoppable. Take it at a lower rank than Season 14 would: its Madawc variant was cut 200% to 100% on the PTR, so it is a utility skill now, not a damage one
  5. Wrath of the Berserker as the ultimate

Rotation

Bank, then swing. Shouts up, Lunging Strike to top the bar, Upheaval at high Fury. Rage of Harrogath's cooldown reduction rate doubled on the PTR (1 second to 0.5), which makes shout uptime cheaper than it was and indirectly supports holding Fury longer between dumps. Melted Heart of Selig's Season 14 zero-Life gearing rule still applies if you run it, but it competes with your Crafted-Mythic budget - and on 3.2.0 the Jeweler route bypasses that budget entirely.

Skill point priority

  1. Upheaval and Lunging Strike maxed
  2. All three shouts maxed for Berserking, Vulnerable, Fortify and damage reduction
  3. Wrath of the Berserker maxed - the flat +30% all damage makes ultimate uptime worth cooldown reduction
  4. Do not invest in Call of the Ancients: its Madawc damage was cut 240% to 120% on the PTR

Rotation

Push honestly aware of the risk: this build's entire case rests on one PTR item change. If Blizzard tunes Ring of Red Furor before Season 15 launches - and a 1%[x] per-Fury bonus with no stated cap is exactly the kind of line that gets tuned - Barbarian has no second answer anywhere in these patch notes and this build falls apart. Test it, enjoy it, and do not plan a season around it until the launch notes confirm it. All values here are 3.2.0 PTR values.

Skill point priority

  1. Upheaval, Lunging Strike and all three shouts maxed
  2. Defensive passives over the last marginal damage nodes - the class lost a lot of baseline damage and gained defensive aspect value, so lean into that rather than fighting it
  3. Wrath of the Berserker uptime prioritised via cooldown reduction

Board & glyph focus

Maximum Fury nodes first - this is the inversion that defines the build. Then Critical Strike Damage, then the surviving damage nodes. Do not route through Blood Rage or Hemorrhage expecting Season 14 value - both were cut on the PTR (60%[x] to 45%[x] and 75%[x] to 60%[x]).

How to run them

[ptr] 3.2.0 changes nothing about mercenaries - the words "Mercenary" and "Mercenaries" and all four mercenary names are absent from the official 3.2.0 notes entirely. So this is the Season 14 pairing carried over, which is the only guidance that exists. Hire Raheir for Valiance's rotating cheat-death and Inspiration's damage multiplier, and reinforce with Varyana on Bloodthirst for attack speed. One PTR-specific point worth making: Barbarian's core stat scalar was cut 1.1 to 0.8, so the class is measurably squishier per point of Strength than it was in Season 14 - if you find yourself dying on the PTR where you did not before, swapping Varyana for Aldkin's Field of Languish is the honest response, and it is a pairing Maxroll already endorses for Barbarian.

Summary

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; every number here comes from the one-week 3.2.0 PTR build and Blizzard routinely revises them. Season 14's number-one class took the patch's heaviest systematic cut - core stat scalar 1.1 to 0.8, four paragon nodes and two glyphs reduced, Two-Handed Sword Expertise bleeding halved - and one item change is why it is still in the top half. Ring of Red Furor's Fury requirement dropped from 100 to 30 and it now grants an additional 1%[x] Bonus Critical Strike Damage per point of Fury above 30. That converts your Fury bar from a spending resource into a damage stat with no stated cap, and it rewards exactly the opposite habit from Season 14's Whirlwind play. Both PTR class tier lists found place Barbarian at A and both name a Ring of Red Furor core pattern as the class's answer; the aggregators name Upheaval and Hammer of the Ancients as the core skills it pairs with. Stated plainly: this is a narrower class position than Season 14's systemic breadth. Barbarian now leans on one ring, and if that ring is tuned down before launch the class has no second answer in these notes.
