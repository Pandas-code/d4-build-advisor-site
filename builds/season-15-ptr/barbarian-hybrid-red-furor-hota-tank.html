fury-banking two-hander (defensive)

Barbarian hybrid unchanged fury-banking two-hander (defensive) based on Ring of Red Furor Upheaval Barbarian PTR

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 15 (PTR)

Move on whenLevel 70 with Torment 1 unlocked and Ring of Red Furor in hand - the same gate as the base build, for the same reason.
Move on whenShout uptime near-continuous and resting Fury sitting well above 30. If shouts are dropping, add Cooldown Reduction before adding anything else.
Move on whenYou can hold ground through a Challenge Dungeon two Torment tiers above your normal difficulty without dying. That is the specific capability this variant buys with its lost clear speed.

Rotation

All three shouts up, Lunging Strike to fill the bar, Hammer of the Ancients when the bar is high. Build the Fury-banking habit from level one; it is unnatural for anyone who played Season 14 Whirlwind and it has to be learned deliberately.

Skill point priority

  1. Hammer of the Ancients to max - the core skill this variant banks Fury for
  2. Lunging Strike for Fury generation, still capped at 0.5 Fury per 1% Maximum Life since 3.1.x
  3. All three shouts taken early rather than late: this variant is built on shout uptime for Berserking, Vulnerable, Fortify and damage reduction together
  4. Wrath of the Berserker as the ultimate for its flat +30% all damage
  5. Skip Leap - the sixth slot goes to the third shout instead, which is the first visible divergence from the base build

  5. No points into Call of the Ancients: its Madawc damage was cut 240% to 120% on the PTR

Rotation

The honest trade at push tiers: this variant clears slower than barbarian-meta-upheaval-red-furor and dies less. In the Pit, where clear time is the score, that is usually the wrong trade and you should push the base build. In Challenge Dungeons scaled three Torment tiers up, in Overland Ambushes, and in Hardcore, it is the right one. It also inherits the base build's central risk unchanged: everything rests on Ring of Red Furor surviving to launch in its current form, and a 1%[x] per-Fury bonus with no stated cap is exactly the kind of line that gets tuned. All values are 3.2.0 PTR values.

Skill point priority

  1. Hammer of the Ancients, Lunging Strike and all three shouts maxed
  2. Defensive passives over every marginal damage node - that is the variant's thesis, not a compromise
  3. Wrath of the Berserker uptime maximised

How to run them

[ptr] 3.2.0 changes nothing about mercenaries - the words "Mercenary" and "Mercenaries" and all four mercenary names are absent from the official 3.2.0 notes entirely. So this is the Season 14 pairing carried over, which is the only guidance that exists. Hire Raheir for Valiance's rotating cheat-death and Inspiration's damage multiplier, and reinforce with Aldkin on Field of Languish for area damage reduction - this is the durability variant, so it takes the defensive branch of the Season 14 either/or rather than the offensive one. One PTR-specific point worth making: Barbarian's core stat scalar was cut 1.1 to 0.8, so the class is measurably squishier per point of Strength than it was in Season 14 - if you find yourself dying on the PTR where you did not before, swapping Varyana for Aldkin's Field of Languish is the honest response, and it is a pairing Maxroll already endorses for Barbarian.

Summary

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; built on 3.2.0 PTR values Blizzard may revise. This is barbarian-meta-upheaval-red-furor rebuilt for the patch's least-discussed detail: while 3.2.0 cut Barbarian's offence hard, it quietly handed the class free defence. Raid Leader's Aspect now grants 7-10% Damage Reduction it never had, and Rage of Harrogath's cooldown reduction rate doubled from 1 second to 0.5. A class that lost 27% of its baseline damage scaling and gained mitigation should probably be played as a durable build rather than as a glass one, which is the opposite of what Season 14 Barbarian became. The variant swaps Upheaval for Hammer of the Ancients - the other core skill both PTR tier sources name alongside Ring of Red Furor - because its slower, heavier rhythm suits banking Fury better than Upheaval's, and it trades some clear speed for the ability to hold ground in Overland Ambushes and Challenge Dungeons, the two new PTR activities that punish squishy melee.

Reasoning

Based onRing of Red Furor Upheaval Barbarian
Tweaks vs. the base build
  1. Core skill swap: Upheaval out, Hammer of the Ancients in. Both are named by both PTR tier sources as Ring of Red Furor cores. Hammer of the Ancients' slower cadence means fewer, larger Fury dumps, which keeps the bar higher on average - and bar height is literally the multiplier under Ring of Red Furor's 1%[x] Bonus Critical Strike Damage per Fury above 30.
  2. Rallying Cry promoted from filler to permanent. Its Madawc damage variant was halved on the PTR so it is no longer a damage skill, but its Fortify and Unstoppable are worth more now, and Rage of Harrogath's doubled cooldown-reduction rate makes keeping it up cheaper than it was in Season 14.
  3. Aspect swap: Aspect of Elemental Fate out, Raid Leader's Aspect promoted to a permanent slot at every stage. Its new 7-10% Damage Reduction is a straight PTR gift and the meta build only picks it up at some stages.
  4. Aspect swap: Aspect of Crushing out, Aspect of Heavenly Strength in at late stages, keeping the two-hander defensive enabler on rather than dropping it for a damage aspect once gear improves.
  5. Paragon reweighting: Maximum Fury first as in the base build - that is non-negotiable, it is the multiplier - but survivability nodes take priority over the second tier of damage nodes rather than the reverse.
  6. Gear reweighting: Maximum Life and Armor rolls are taken over the third damage affix on every slot. The base build takes damage there.
  7. Soul Splinter: still Splinter of Anguish over Splinter of Hellfire, for the same reason as the base build - Ring of Red Furor pays out in Bonus Critical Strike Damage and Hellfire's -100% Critical Strike Chance would mean never collecting it. Splinter of Pain is the one genuine alternative here and only here: 10-40% Damage Reduction for -30% Movement Speed is a trade a deliberately durable melee build can actually make, where the base build cannot. Test both.
  8. This variant is not faster. If you want clear speed, play the base build; this one exists to survive content that kills the base build.
