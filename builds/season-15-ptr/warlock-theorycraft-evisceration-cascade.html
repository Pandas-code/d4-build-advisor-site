Evisceration Cascade Warlock

eviscerate-proc summoner

Warlock theorycraft unchanged eviscerate-proc summoner confidence medium PTR

Last verified 2026-08-08 • Season 15 (PTR)

Move on whenLevel 70, Torment 1, and Nails of the Gore-Crowned in hand. Before that item this is just a worse Dread Claws build and there is no reason to run it over the meta.
Move on whenInfernal Homunculus equipped and demon uptime visibly continuous. If demon uptime is still patchy with Infernal Homunculus on, stop here and go play warlock-meta-blazing-scream - the hypothesis has failed its first real test.
Move on whenYou have measured Eviscerate proc rate and it is high enough that Evisceration Fragment is visibly your top damage source. If it is not, this build does not graduate to pushing - that is the honest exit condition.

Skill bar

1Dread Claws
2Command Fallen
3Umbral Chains
4Wall of Agony
5Blazing Scream
6Apocalypse

Rotation

Command Fallen to build a demon count, Umbral Chains to pull, Dread Claws on the pack. Blazing Scream only when there is nothing better to press. Watch how often you see Eviscerate fire at this stage - if it is rare here with only the base 2 to 5 percent chance, that is expected; the build does not exist until Nails of the Gore-Crowned does.

Skill point priority

  1. Dread Claws first and hardest - Ravenous Jaws is the Eviscerate source you have before the uniques exist
  2. Command Fallen (Fallen Rush) to keep demon count up, which is the resource this build actually spends
  3. Umbral Chains for grouping so demons hit multiple targets
  4. Wall of Agony (Encircle) while your Dominance pool is small
  5. Blazing Scream as filler damage - it is not the plan here, just something to press
  6. Apocalypse as the ultimate

Rotation

This is the stage where you collect the data the hypothesis needs. Keep Command Fallen on cooldown, group with Umbral Chains, and watch the combat log for Evisceration Fragment procs. Note the Misanthropic Aspect conflict up front: it grants 25-45% Damage Reduction only when at most one Demon has been alive for over two seconds, which is the exact opposite of what this build wants. It is listed here because the early stage genuinely has low demon uptime; drop it the moment demon count is stable.

Skill point priority

  1. Dread Claws maxed for Ravenous Jaws' 500% Eviscerate damage
  2. Command Fallen maxed - demon count is the throughput variable in the hypothesis
  3. Metamorphosis added here: on the PTR the Destruction Demon applies Burning with any skill cast, so it stops requiring a dedicated cast to be useful
  4. Umbral Chains for grouping
  5. Blazing Scream kept low - it is a resource dump, not the plan
  6. Apocalypse as the ultimate

Rotation

Command Fallen and Metamorphosis on cooldown to keep bodies out, Umbral Chains to hold the pack inside the demons, Dread Claws for the Ravenous Jaws Eviscerate, Bombardment while moving. Then stop and read the numbers: this build is being run to answer a question, not because anyone has proven it works.

Skill point priority

  4. Umbral Chains for grouping
  5. Remaining points into Shadow and Hellfire passives

Rotation

Untested at push tiers by definition. The predicted failure mode is bossing: a single target gives demons fewer attacks, so Eviscerate procs thin out exactly when you need them most, and Gauntlets of Sheol's sacrifice route may simply be the better boss answer even though it contradicts the rest of the build. Push only after the late-endgame measurement passes, and expect to swap to warlock-meta-blazing-scream for boss-heavy content. All of this is PTR behaviour and may not survive to Season 15 launch.

Skill point priority

Summary

PTR - subject to change before Season 15 launches; this build is untested theorycraft built on 3.2.0 PTR numbers that Blizzard may revise before release. Three separate Eviscerate buffs landed in the same patch and nobody appears to have connected them yet: Legion Shard's Evisceration Fragment went from 200% to 1000% damage with its Lesser Demon Eviscerate chance raised 2% to 5%, Dread Claws' Ravenous Jaws Eviscerate damage went 125% to 500%, and the unique Nails of the Gore-Crowned now gains 0.5-2.5% Eviscerate chance per second while making Hellion Sting burst on Eviscerate. The build stops treating Eviscerate as an execute and treats it as a proc engine: keep the demon count high, keep Eviscerate chance climbing, and let the 1000% Evisceration Fragment fire constantly instead of occasionally. Be clear that this is a hypothesis, not a tested build - no source recommends it and it may simply not have the demon throughput to sustain the chance stacking.

Reasoning

HypothesisEviscerate chance is now stackable from a per-second source (Nails of the Gore-Crowned, 0.5-2.5%/second) rather than only from flat item lines, and the two Eviscerate payloads were buffed by 5x and 4x in the same patch. If Eviscerate chance can be driven high enough that most Lesser Demon attacks proc, Legion Shard's Evisceration Fragment at 1000% becomes the build's primary damage source rather than a bonus - which would beat Blazing Scream on packs, because it scales with the number of demons hitting things rather than with how many times you cast.
Confidencemedium
How to test itTest in this order. (1) Sit still with Nails of the Gore-Crowned equipped and no combat for 60 seconds, then check the character sheet: does Eviscerate chance actually accumulate out of combat, and does it cap? The 0.5-2.5% per second wording does not state a cap and that is the single number the whole hypothesis rests on. (2) With Infernal Homunculus on, confirm the free Lesser Demon Upgrades at 200% duration keep demon count high enough that Evisceration Fragment has bodies to proc from - if demon uptime is the bottleneck the build fails regardless of chance. (3) Compare single-target Pit boss damage against warlock-meta-blazing-scream at equal gear; the hypothesis predicts this build wins on packs and loses badly on bosses, and if it also loses on packs, abandon it. (4) Check whether Gauntlets of Sheol's sacrifice mechanic (consume Archfiend summons while a Fiend is alive for 40-50% Fiend damage per demon, up to 600-750%) competes with keeping demons alive for Eviscerate procs - the two want opposite things and only one can be right.
