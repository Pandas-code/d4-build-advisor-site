Every entry keeps the game database’s own wording, with its numbers resolved from the game database’s own roll formulas at maximum item power — a range like 13 – 25% is the whole span the affix can roll. Skill and aspect damage is quoted the way the game scales it, as a percentage of your weapon damage (175% damage means one weapon hit and three quarters), for the base skill at rank 1. Where a value depends on something the database does not carry — your Maximum Life, your character level, your attack speed — the placeholder stays: {x} — this site never invents the number behind it. A few of those are filled in from published tables instead, and each one names its source.

Recipes lists every crafting recipe in the game today (1,840), with the 117 tempering tiers folded into their 39 manuals. 221 of them have an ingredient list the game data leaves empty or open, and each says so on its entry rather than guessing. 89 have no name in the game and no output to be named by, so they keep their game key. 2 test recipes the game ships by mistake are left out. Which crafter and tab a recipe sits under, and salvage yields, are not read out of the game yet, so neither is shown.
