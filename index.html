Class RankingSeason 14

S
Barbarian★
The only unanimous pick. TierListHQ's 2026-08-05 snapshot (the freshest class read, taken on patch 3.1.2) rates it S+ and calls it 'the clean #1 of the settled meta'; Maxroll's overall endgame list gives Barbarian three of the four S-tier builds (Whirlwind, Mighty Throw, Minion) and its push list five (adding Rend and Flame Charge); Icy Veins rates it S for both speed farming and endgame push. The strength is systemic build breadth, not one outlier skill — it absorbed real 3.1.0 nerfs (Fury generation capped at 0.5 per 1% max Life and cut off from Resource Generation bonuses, Aspect of Limitless Rage gutted from 1-1.5% to 0.15-0.20% damage per Fury) and still leads. Only dissent: Icy Veins rates it B for levelling, so early Barbarian is slow.
Rogue★
Consensus #2. TierListHQ calls it 'clear second place' with Poison Penetrating Shot now leading the class; Icy Veins is the only class to rate S across all three activities (levelling, speed farming, endgame push); Maxroll's push list has both Rain of Arrows and Penetrating Shot in S with six more Rogue builds in A/B. Maxroll's week-1 read: 'exceptionally balanced across multiple builds', with Cold Rain of Arrows and Poison Penetrating Shot leading Tower leaderboards. Documented disagreement: Maxroll's overall endgame list places no Rogue build in S (five sit in A), so Rogue reads as a pushing and leaderboard class more than a low-effort farming class. It took a Dance of Knives nerf (65% to 52%) but gained Flurry (140% to 196%) and Twisting Blades (30%/70% to 42%/98%).
Druid★
Third overall on the freshest snapshot, with 'the broadest build variety'. Icy Veins rates it S across levelling, speed farming and endgame push and puts Shred and Lightning Storm in S tier; Maxroll's push list has Shred in S. The rise is item-driven rather than skill-tree-driven: the Berú of the Storm Shepherd set makes damage scale directly with Spirit regeneration rate, and 3.1.0 buffed Waxing Gibbous (20-25% to 45-60% damage, 25% to 33% extra-attack chance), Hunter's Zenith (40-60% to 75-90%) and Archdruid's Aspect (up to 45-60%). Two honest caveats recorded in knowledge: survivability is the class's named weakness, and Maxroll attributes part of the Storm Shepherd dominance to bugs (including the click-don't-hold Shred interaction), so this is the highest nerf-risk S-tier class of the three.
A
Spiritborn
The most source-contested class in the top half. Maxroll's overall endgame list rates Evade Counterswarm S — one of only four S-tier builds and the only non-Barbarian one — and its push list agrees; Icy Veins rates the class S for levelling and speed farming. But TierListHQ's fresher 3.1.2 snapshot holds it at A because it 'excels at speed with Evade Counterswarm but weaker at bossing', and Maxroll's own week-1 note says a Poison Stinger bug is doing part of the work. Withering Fist is the sharpest per-build contradiction in the season's data: S tier on Icy Veins, D tier on Maxroll. A rather than S because two of its strong showings rest on a bug and a single build, and its bossing is a known hole.
Sorcerer
A one-build class with an exceptional build. Maxroll's week-1 review says Firewall 'outperforms every other build by at least one order of magnitude' in raw DPS while being slow and clunky, and Maxroll's push list rates it S — yet the same site's overall endgame list keeps it at A, and TierListHQ places the class at A calling it 'settled into upper-mid tier'. The spread inside the class is the widest of any: Ball Lightning and Ice Shards are fine, while Hydra, Frozen Orb and Meteor sit in D tier on both Maxroll lists. Icy Veins rates it S for speed farming only. 3.1.0 gave it real tools (Fundamental Release 10% to 30% per stack up to 90%, Icefall's +50% to Frost skills, Cain's Wild Lightning cap 400% to 750%, softer Habacalva's Cauldron heat decay) but also diminishing returns on Unstable Currents and a Fractured Winterglass cut to 2-3%.
Necromancer
Bottom of the A band. Every source agrees the ceiling is A and nothing more: TierListHQ says 'Blood Wave overperforms, though limited build depth behind it'; Maxroll's overall endgame list has Blood Wave in A and nothing higher; Icy Veins is the outlier that puts Naz Mages and Blood Wave in S, but that list predates patches 3.1.1 and 3.1.2. Icy Veins also gives it B for speed farming, its worst activity mark for any class. Sources even disagree on which build carries it — Maxroll's week-1 says Bone Spirit reclaimed the top spot, the fresher snapshot names Blood Wave. Red Blessing's max Overpower bonus dropping from 4 to 2 pushed the Overpower archetypes down and left AFK-friendly Zoo/Golem play as the fallback.
B
Paladin
Blizzard buffed it hard at 3.1.0 — every Oath went up (Judicator, Juggernaut and Disciple all to 80%), Wing Strikes 160% to 200%, Defiance Aura 30% to 50%, Aegis now adds 30% Armor — and the freshest snapshot still records that the 'launch buffs didn't translate to Pit performance'. Maxroll's best Paladin entry on the overall endgame list is Shield Charge in A, with five builds in C and Zenith and Judgement in D. Its only S-tier Paladin entry anywhere is an explicitly group-only Support zDPS build, which is the honest read of the class's top-end role this season. Icy Veins dissents sharply (S for levelling and speed farming, two S-tier Paladin builds) but that list is dated 2026-07-04, before the meta settled. Genuinely good at what it is: tanky, Hardcore-friendly, valuable in groups.
Warlock
Received the largest buff pass of the patch and finished last anyway — the snapshot's phrasing is 'fun does not equal meta'. Legion Shard's Spawn Fragment explosion doubled (75% to 150%), Apocalypse's survivor damage went 10% to 25%, Moloch's Beating Flame 8-11% to 20-30%, and several summon durations were extended, yet Maxroll has no Warlock build above B on its overall endgame list. It pushes better than it farms — Maxroll's push list carries Apocalypse, Lunatic, Dread Claws and Blazing Scream in A, and Blazing Scream took Rank 1 on the SSF leaderboard. Icy Veins lists three S-tier Warlock builds, but that is the widest source disagreement of the season and its list predates 3.1.1/3.1.2; knowledge/classes/warlock.md flags any S-tier Warlock claim as contested and unsuitable as load-bearing justification. Also the most mechanically complex class in the game (dual Wrath/Dominance resources), which raises the floor to reach its ceiling.

werewolf melee / storm hybrid
Druidunchanged

The Druid meta build, and the clearest example this season of an item-driven rather than skill-driven meta. Shred's damage is carried by the Berú of the Storm Shepherd set, whose bonus scales damage directly with your Spirit regeneration rate - which means regen affixes are damage affixes and the whole gearing logic inverts from a normal build. Cross-checked: Icy Veins rates Shred S tier, Maxroll's push list rates it S tier, and the freshest patch-3.1.2 class snapshot says Storm Shred remains viable after the 3.1.1 adjustments. Perma-Berserk Rend Barbarian

melee bleed / cooldown-uptime
Barbarianunchangedconfidence medium

UNTESTED - this is an original theorycraft, not a build any source has ranked in this exact form. The idea: 3.1.0 both capped Fury generation (0.5 Fury per 1% Maximum Life, and no longer helped by Resource Generation bonuses) and gutted Aspect of Limitless Rage from 1-1.5% to 0.15-0.20% damage per Fury, while simultaneously turning Wrath of the Berserker into a flat +30% all damage for its duration. Those two changes point in opposite directions, and every published Barbarian build still gears for Fury. This build gears for cooldown reduction instead and pairs it with Rend, a bleed that ticks over time and therefore spends more of its damage inside a persistent multiplier window than a burst skill would. Rend is independently credible - Maxroll has it S tier on the push list and A tier on the overall endgame list, and its week-1 review notes Rend unexpectedly dominating Tower leaderboards - but the cooldown-first gearing is mine and has not been validated by anyone.

Bug-Proof Storm Regen Lightning Druid

ranged storm caster, Spirit-regeneration scaling
Druidunchangedconfidence medium

UNTESTED - original theorycraft built as deliberate insurance rather than as a damage maximiser. The meta Shred build is excellent right now but rests on two things Maxroll itself flags as bugs: the Storm Shepherd set's dominance is partly attributed to bugs, and clicking Shred rather than holding it re-triggers the Waxing Gibbous 2-set bonus, which Maxroll calls a mechanical exploit. Exploits get patched. The question this build asks is whether the durable part of the Druid meta - the Berú of the Storm Shepherd rule that damage scales with Spirit regeneration rate - can be harvested by a Storm skill that does not depend on the Shred click interaction at all. Lightning Storm is the candidate: Icy Veins independently rates it S tier, it is already in the meta build's own skill bar as an Orange Herald trigger, and it is a ranged toggle, which also sidesteps Druid's named survivability weakness. If the Shred interaction is patched, this build should be roughly unaffected; if it is not, this build is probably a modest downgrade. That trade is the entire point.
